
##	Networks

- a network is a collection of devices connected with each other to share data and resourses

- a client is a device that accesses a service made available / served by the server 

- a server is a device that provides functions or servises for the clients

- the cloud is a collection of routers that makes the internet


##	Switches

- switches connect endhosts together forming a LAN(Local Area Network) 

- switches has 24 or more ports 

- switches can't connect LANs


##	Routers

- Routers are used to connect LANs to the internet

- routers has fewer ports  


##	network firewalls

- firewall are devices used to protect networks from cyber attacks

- Firewalls can stand before or after a Router

- firewalls use rules to allow good trafic and deny susspecious trafic

- newer fire walls are called net-generation firewalls , the provide modern security mesurements 


##	host-basd firewalls

- they are software applications that filterentering and exiting trafic to the host pc
