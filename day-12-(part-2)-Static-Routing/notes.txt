although similair to default Gateway , default Route is for Routers

instead of dropping packets ,they go through the default route when there are no matches found

usually made for Routers that have access to outer Internet

since the Routers choose the most specific route that matches 

the Default Route must be the opposite - The Least specific route - 

and the least specific is  0.0.0.0/0 including every single route

all the non matching routes pour in the 0.0.0.0/0 , and get Forwarded to the Internet
