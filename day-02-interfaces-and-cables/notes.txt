						FIBER


for larger Networks we use Fiber optics instead of copper

to plug it to a switch we need a -SFP- (small form-factor pluggable) 

the fiber optic cable uses two wires one for transmition and the other for receiving

         sfp (TX) ------------------------------ (Rx) sfp 
switch                                                      switch
         sfp (RX) ------------------------------ (Tx) sfp


the cable itself has 4 layers
1/ the glass core that transmits light
2/ cladding that reflects light
3/ a protctive buffer,(protect from breaking)
4/ the outer jacket


there are two types of fiber cables 

1- multi mode 
allows multiple light signals (modes) to travel at once with different angles
the cabbles are cheaper , and use LED based SFPs 
it has a wider glass core 

2-single mode
allows a single mode to be transmitted, and it's sent by a Laser SFP
the cable is more expensive than multi mode
it has a narrow glass core and a wider cladding for protection



    speed        |         cable type        |     standard    |     informal name   |    maximum length

## Q1 - you connect tow old routers together with a UTP cable , however data is not successfully sent and received between them. what could be the problem

1/ they are connected with a straight-through cable 
2/ they are connected with a cross-over cable
3/ they are operating in auto-MDIX mode


my answer is            1/ they are connected with a straight-through cable 

solution : ✅ to connect with the same device type you need a cross-over cable , or have auto-MDIX enabled, which in this case can't do
